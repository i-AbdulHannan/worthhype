The reputation gap

Why great businesses still lose customers

Doing excellent work is not enough if nobody can prove it. Here is what quietly kills local trust.

Asking is awkward

Owners verbally beg for a review and forget. Busy clients leave and completely forget about it too.

Fake reviews plague platforms

Anyone can post anything on open review platforms. Genuine 5-star ratings get drowned out by unverified noise.

Silent public complaints

Unhappy clients rarely complain directly. They vent via a public 1-star review before you ever get a chance to fix it.

The worthhype solution turns customer happiness into verified public trust, automatically.

Route every real customer through a secure tokenized WhatsApp request. Verified reviews go public with an official badge. Complaints stay private until you resolve them.
